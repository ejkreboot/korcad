Tool settings

Safe Z is the raised travel height above the material. It must clear warped stock and cut pieces.

Blade offset is the distance from the knife swivel axis to its tip. The generator rounds spindle motion around corners so the trailing tip follows the design path.

Up folds with selects whether Up-fold paths receive drag-knife compensation or follow the centerline with a creasing wheel. Down folds are scored with the drag knife in the cut program. Both tools keep the spindle off.

Overcut continues past the start of a closed cut to sever fibers left by compression or blade lag.

Corner relief removes a narrow slot between adjacent beveled panels so they do not bind when folded.

Bit diameter offsets the deck perimeter outward and each opening inward by half the entered diameter. This is geometric compensation; verify actual kerf with a calibration cut.

Fold allowance shortens every folded panel by one bend deduction so the finished part reaches its drawn size. For a 90° fold, BD = 2(r + t) − (π/2)(r + K·t), where t is board thickness, r is the crease radius and K places the neutral axis. The deduction is taken from the child panel, so deck faces and pocket openings keep their drawn dimensions. Cut the calibration coupon to measure the real value for your board.
